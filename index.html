Product & architecture guide

SnatchPicks

A community-driven, mobile-first deal platform for Indian shoppers. People and partner feeds post deals, an automated pipeline weeds out fakes, the community votes the best ones up, and shoppers get alerted the moment something they care about drops.

🇮🇳 India only · INR · en_IN 📱 Mobile-first web + native app 🗳️ Community-ranked 🤖 AI-assisted moderation 🔔 Real-time alerts
Brand promise: “Every deal verified. No fake MRP. Ever.” Most deal sites are a firehose of inflated “was ₹9,999, now ₹999” listings. SnatchPicks treats trust as the product: every submission passes safety, duplicate and price-sanity checks, and the community keeps confirming whether a deal still works.
🎯

Who it's for

Gen-Z and young deal-hunting shoppers in India who live on their phones and discover products through Instagram and Telegram.

🪜

Niche-first launch

Beauty & skincare → fashion → baby & parenting: the same shopper across her life stages, instead of going broad and diluting the cold start.

📣

Two growth engines

Instagram + Telegram for fast traffic from day one; Google/SEO on real deal, store and category pages for compounding growth.

💸

Business model

Bootstrapped on affiliate revenue: outbound clicks to Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa and partner networks carry affiliate tags.

3The core flow

SnatchPicks is a loop: deals come in, get checked, get ranked by the community, and get pushed to the people who want them. Every click out feeds the affiliate model that funds the loop.

  1. 🔎

    Found

    A shopper spots a deal, or a partner feed surfaces one.

  2. 📝

    Posted

    Submitted on web or app, or imported by the scraper.

  3. 🛡️

    Checked

    Safety, duplicate, fake-MRP and AI checks, plus human review when unsure.

  4. ✅

    Live

    Appears in feeds, search and store/category pages.

  5. 🗳️

    Judged

    The community votes, comments and confirms it still works.

  6. 🔥

    Ranked

    The hot score pushes the best fresh deals to the top.

  7. 🔔

    Delivered

    Alerts, digests, pushes and Telegram reach interested shoppers.

  8. 🛒

    Bought

    The shopper clicks out to the store; affiliate revenue funds the loop, and back to step 1.

Three typical journeys

🛍️ The shopper

  1. Opens the Hot feed or gets a push for an alert.
  2. Opens a deal: sees real price, original price, coupon, price history.
  3. Saves it, votes, or clicks out to buy.
  4. Gets nudged if a saved deal is about to expire.

✍️ The contributor

  1. Signs in and submits a deal (up to 3 per hour).
  2. Automated checks approve, reject or queue it within seconds.
  3. Upvotes raise the deal's rank and the poster's reputation.
  4. At milestones (5, 10, 25… upvotes) they get notified.

🧑‍⚖️ The admin

  1. Reviews submissions the AI wasn't sure about.
  2. Reviews scraped deals, picks a category, approves or rejects.
  3. Handles reports, features deals, sets deal of the day.
  4. Watches analytics and scraper runs.

4System architecture

One Next.js application on Vercel is both the website and the backend API. Every client (browser, native app, extension) talks to it. A single Postgres database is the source of truth. Background work runs as Vercel cron jobs, and deal ingestion runs as a separate Node scraper on GitHub Actions.

Components

ComponentWhereResponsibility
Web app & APIapps/web~66 pages and ~66 API route handlers (App Router). Renders the site, serves JSON to the app and extension, runs moderation, sends notifications.
Middlewareapps/web/src/middleware.tsRuns on every request at the edge: checks the signed session token for admin areas, applies per-IP rate limits to write endpoints, and converts the mobile app's Bearer token into a normal session.
Cron jobsapps/web/vercel.jsonEight scheduled HTTP endpoints (see Alerts) protected by a shared secret.
Databasepackages/databasePrisma schema (33 models, 11 enums), versioned migrations and seed data. Shared by web and scraper.
Scraperpackages/scraperPulls offers from partner/affiliate feeds into a review queue. Runs on GitHub Actions.
Native appapps/mobileExpo / React Native client. Standalone install (React 18) so it doesn't clash with the web app's React 19.
Extensionapps/extensionChrome MV3 content script + popup for 19 Indian (and South-Asian) retailer sites.
Marketing pageapps/marketingStatic single-file landing page deployed separately.
Shared configpackages/configShared TypeScript, ESLint and Tailwind presets.

Design decisions worth knowing

  • One backend. The website is the API. The native app and extension are thin clients, so business rules live in one place.
  • JWT sessions + edge checks. Admin access is enforced centrally in middleware from the signed token (no DB hit), and again in each admin route.
  • Mobile uses a Bearer token, not cookies. Cookies from an in-app browser aren't visible to native requests, so the app receives the session token once and sends it as a header (see Apps).
  • Scraped deals never go live automatically. They land in a review queue; a human picks the category and approves.
  • Data-driven SEO only. About 6,000 templated “Best X in India” pages were deliberately removed; SEO now comes from real deal, store, category and intent pages generated from the database.
  • India first. The schema can represent other South-Asian countries, but no feature assumes multi-country support yet.

5Deal lifecycle

Deals enter through two doors, user submissions and the scraper, and both end up as the same Deal record in the public feed.

States a deal moves through

Door 1 — user submission & automated moderation

CheckRuleOutcome on failure
URL safetyGoogle Safe Browsing: malware, phishing, unwanted software.Auto-reject
DuplicateSame store URL (ignoring utm_*, ref, tag, aff) already live or pending.Auto-reject
Price sanityNegative price · deal price above original · discount over 99% · original over 5× the price on items under ₹10,000 (MRP inflation) · over 90% off on items under ₹50,000.Human review
AI checkgpt-4o-mini classifies title, description and URL as legit / spam / malicious / unclear with a confidence score. Auto-decision only at ≥ 0.82.Reject, or human review if unsure

The AI also writes a short aiSummary shown on the deal page.

Door 2 — scraper ingestion & admin review

AdapterSourceWhat it pulls
amazonKeepa deals API (Amazon.in)Top 50 price drops of 20%+; prices converted from paise; affiliate tag appended.
flipkartFlipkart Affiliate APITop 80 items from the “best offers” feed.
myntraMyntra affiliate JSON feedUp to 80 products.
cuelinksCuelinks Offers APIUp to 100 affiliate offers across merchants.
inrdealsINRDeals Deal Feed APIUp to 100 affiliate deals across merchants.

Every adapter shares the same safeguards: HTTP retries with exponential backoff on rate limits and server errors, a price ceiling to catch mis-parsed values, original prices kept only when they're actually higher than the deal price, and de-duplication by (source, externalId) and against URLs that are already live. Re-running updates prices on existing queue items instead of creating duplicates.

6Ranking & community trust

The Hot feed rewards deals that collect votes quickly and lets them fade with age, so fresh, genuinely good deals rise to the top.

Hot score hotScore = (upvotes − downvotes) / (ageInHours + 2)1.5
Updated on every vote and recomputed for all live deals every hour.
💥

LOOT

hot score > 5. Users who saved the deal get a “just went LOOT” push when it crosses 2.

🔥

HOT

hot score > 2

☀️

WARM

hot score > 0.5

✈️

Telegram

Auto-posted to the channel the first time its hot score reaches 10.

Trust signals on a deal

  • Verified: deals an admin approves from the scraper review queue are marked verified.
  • Honest pricing: the discount badge is withheld when the claimed discount looks implausible (≥ 80%).
  • Price history: every price change is recorded, so “all-time low” claims can be checked.
  • Still working?: live WORKING vs EXPIRED counts, plus coupon-verified counts.
  • Reputation: contributors build reputation from upvotes (and referrals), shown on the leaderboard.

Pipeline

Operations & safeguards

🩺

Observability

Sentry in the web app (server, edge, client) and in the scraper; every cron job reports failures.

🚦

Rate limiting

Upstash Redis sliding windows per IP on write endpoints (votes, comments, submissions, saves, requests), plus per-user submission caps.

🔐

Security headers

Content-Security-Policy, HSTS, frame denial, strict referrer and permissions policies on every response.

✅

Config validation

Environment configuration is validated with Zod at startup, so a bad deploy fails fast.

🧭

SEO hygiene

Sitemap and footer generated from the filesystem and database; legacy templated paths are marked noindex.

13Glossary

LOOT / HOT / WARMBadges for hot scores above 5, 2 and 0.5.
YMMV“Your mileage may vary”: the deal may not work for everyone (location, account, card).
Status voteA WORKING / EXPIRED vote, separate from 👍/👎, used to retire dead deals.

Bank offerExtra instant discount for paying with a specific bank's card.
StackingCombining a deal with a coupon, bank offer and cashback for the lowest final price.
