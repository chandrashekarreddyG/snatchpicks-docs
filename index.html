Product & architecture guide

SnatchPicks

A community-driven, mobile-first deal platform for Indian shoppers. People and partner feeds post deals, an automated pipeline weeds out fakes, the community votes the best ones up, and shoppers get alerted when something they care about drops. It launches beauty & skincare first, then fashion, then baby & parenting.

🇮🇳 India only · INR · en_IN 💄 Beauty-first 📱 Mobile-first web + native app 🗳️ Community-ranked 🤖 AI-assisted moderation
Brand promise: “Every deal verified. No fake MRP. Ever.” Most deal sites are a firehose of inflated “was ₹9,999, now ₹999” listings. SnatchPicks treats trust as the product: submissions pass safety, duplicate and price-sanity checks, and the community keeps confirming whether a deal still works.
🎯

Who it's for

Gen-Z and young deal-hunting shoppers in India who live on their phones and discover products through Instagram and Telegram.

🪜

Niche-first launch

Beauty & skincare → fashion → baby & parenting: the same shopper across her life stages, instead of going broad and diluting the cold start.

📣

Two growth engines

Instagram + Telegram for traffic from day one; Google/SEO on real deal, store and category pages for compounding growth.

💸

Business model

Bootstrapped on affiliate revenue: outbound clicks to stores such as Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and Nykaa carry affiliate tags.

3The core loop

SnatchPicks is a loop: deals come in, get checked, get ranked by the community, and get pushed to the people who want them. Every click out feeds the affiliate model that funds the loop.

  1. 🔎

    Found

    A shopper spots a deal, or a partner feed surfaces one.

  2. 📝

    Posted

    Submitted on web or app, or imported by the scraper.

  3. 🛡️

    Checked

    Safety, duplicate, fake-MRP and AI checks, plus human review when unsure.

  4. ✅

    Live

    Appears in feeds, search and store/category pages.

  5. 🗳️

    Judged

    The community votes, comments and confirms it still works.

  6. 🔥

    Ranked

    The hot score pushes the best fresh deals to the top.

  7. 🔔

    Delivered

    Alerts, digests, pushes and Telegram reach interested shoppers.

  8. 🛒

    Bought

    The shopper clicks out to the store; affiliate revenue funds the loop, and back to step 1.
